Properties & winning+

Group your faceup properties by color. A complete set has the number of cards shown in its rent table. Custom colors and destination names can differ from the classic deck, so use the numbers on your cards.

  • Have 3 complete sets in 3 different colors to win. Two sets of the same color do not count as two winning sets.
  • You can own additional properties and additional sets. There is no seven-card limit on the table.
  • You may rearrange your property collection on your turn. Cards on the table never go back into your hand.
  • A full set is protected from Sly Deal and from having one of its cards taken by Forced Deal. Dealbreaker can take the whole set, and you may still use properties from a full set to pay a debt.
Wild Property cards+

A two-color wild counts as one property of either color printed on it. Turn it so the chosen color is clear. You can change its color or move it between sets on your own turn, without spending one of your three card plays.

A complete set must include at least one standard (non-wild) Property card. The rainbow Wild Property can join any color set, but needs another property with it; it cannot make a set by itself. It has no cash value, so you cannot bank it or use it to settle a debt. Two-color wilds have the cash value printed on them.

Wilds can be taken by Sly Deal or Forced Deal when they are outside a full set. They travel with a full set taken by Dealbreaker.

Banking & paying other players+
  • Bank money cards or action cards for their printed cash value. Each deposit uses one card play. Properties never go in the bank.
  • A banked action can no longer perform its action, even after another player receives it as payment.
  • Pay with your bank, your faceup properties, or a combination. The payer chooses which cards to hand over, using their cash values, not their rent values.
  • There is no change. Overpay if you cannot make the amount exactly.
  • If you cannot cover the bill, pay what you can from the table. You owe no remaining balance. If you have nothing payable, you pay nothing.
  • Never pay with cards in your hand. Money received goes into your bank; properties received go into your property collection.
Pay Up & Double Fare+

Pay Up with two colors: choose one of the pictured colors that you own and charge every other player the rent for that set. You do not need a complete set.

Pay Up with the rainbow: choose any one color you own and charge just one player. Read the card’s text to tell the versions apart.

Use the rent row for how many properties are in the chosen set. Add any valid House and Hotel bonuses. Charge for one set only, not the total of all your properties.

Double Fare doubles that rent. Rent + one Double Fare uses 2 plays. Rent + two Double Fare cards uses all 3 plays and charges 4× rent. Announce them together. Double Fare cannot multiply Overweight Baggage or It’s Our Anniversary.

Houses & Hotels+

Add a House only to a complete property set to raise its rent by $3M. A Hotel adds another $4M and requires a House first; keep both on the set.

  • Each set may have at most one House and one Hotel.
  • Do not build on Railroad or Utility sets, including their custom equivalents.
  • Playing a House or Hotel uses one of your three plays. You can bank it instead for its printed cash value.
  • A Dealbreaker takes the complete set with its House and Hotel.

Example: a complete Naoshima set charges $7M. Add a House for $10M, then a Hotel for $14M. One Double Fare makes that $28M.

Stealing, swapping & saying no+

Sly Deal: take one opponent’s property that is not in a complete set. Forced Deal: swap one of your properties for one opponent’s property that is not in their complete set. Received properties go straight into your collection.

Dealbreaker: take one complete set, including its wilds and buildings. It uses one card play.

Fuck No: play it from your hand in response to an action against you, even during someone else’s turn. It protects you, not the whole table. Another Fuck No can cancel yours; continue if someone has another.

Using Fuck No to block an action does not count toward your three plays, even on your own turn. Banking it as money still uses a play. You cannot respond with one that is already in your bank.

Quick questions+

What if the draw pile runs out?

Shuffle the discard pile to make a new draw pile. Leave everyone’s hands, banks, and properties where they are.

What if I use my last card?

Wait until your next turn. If your hand is still empty then, draw 5 instead of 2. Drawing those 5 does not use any of your three plays.

Does Free Ticket use a play?

Yes. It uses one play to draw two more cards. You can use the new cards with any plays you still have left.

Can I trade freely with someone?

Use the action cards to transfer properties. Ordinary table trades and loans are not part of this game’s rules.

Does everyone pay on It’s Our Anniversary?

Every other player owes you $2M. You do not pay yourself. Each payer can respond with Fuck No.
